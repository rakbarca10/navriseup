Fueling Higher Education: 12th Grade Book Distribution in Aurangabad

Event Details
  • Date: August 19, 2026
  • Location: Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar), Maharashtra
  • Beneficiaries: 12th Class High School Students
Support Right When It Matters Most

Heading into the 12th grade is one of the most critical turning points in a student's academic life, determining college admissions and future career paths. Navriseup India Care Foundation organized an essential book distribution drive in Aurangabad, providing comprehensive 12th-grade textbooks and reference guides right when students needed them most.

Enabling Academic Dreams to Flourish

Higher secondary reference books can be prohibitively expensive for low-income households. By directly providing required syllabi books, we lift a substantial financial burden off hardworking families and ensure bright young scholars can prepare for their board examinations without compromise.

Backing Students at Critical Milestones

Targeted higher education support creates long-term social mobility:

  • Equal Exam Preparation: Quality textbooks give students every advantage in scoring high marks in board examinations.
  • Alleviating Household Debt: Preventing families from borrowing high-interest loans for school study materials.
  • Inspiring Future Ambitions: Showing students that an entire community stands behind their aspirations for college and beyond.
Invest in Young Minds Today

Help us ensure that financial constraints never force a talented student to drop out:

  • Donate: Sponsor complete sets of board exam books and tuition guides for deserving students.
  • Volunteer: Mentor 10th and 12th grade students through study techniques and exam preparation.
  • Share: Help spread the word to connect more students with educational scholarships and materials.
