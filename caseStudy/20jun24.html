Provision of Fulfilling Snacks for Zila Parishad School, Aurangabad

Event Details
  • Objective: Enhancing productivity and well-being among 100 children at Zilla Parishad School, Aurangabad, through the provision of nourishing snacks. In rural areas like Aurangabad, access to nutritious snacks can significantly impact children's productivity and health. Our initiative aims to provide fulfilling snacks to 100 students, ensuring they receive adequate nutrition during school hours. This report outlines the implementation, expected outcomes, impact assessment, and sustainability of this effort.
  • Date: Jun 20th, 2024
  • Targeted Distribution: Snacks such as patties, chips, and lemon juice provided to students during school breaks.

Impact
  • Cost Alleviation: Curbing the high cost of education by providing essential stationery directly to students.
  • Productivity Enhancement: Improved student productivity through access to necessary educational tools.
  • Preventing Drop-Outs: It is usually seen in the underprivileged section of our society that children drop-out in early stages of school.

It is due to the extra-costs involved in education related to educational tools and resources that families can't bear.

Previous Outreach Impact:
  • Technology Integration: Sponsorship of a Smart TV in the previous outreach initiative.
  • Continued Support: Ensuring regular support for educational needs of students in Aurangabad.
Benefit Enhancement:

Snack Provision: Provided fulfilling snacks (patties, chips, lemon juice, etc.) to 80 kids, promoting better productivity during their educational activities.

Conclusion

The outreach initiative aims to alleviate educational expenses and enhance productivity among students. Continued support, including snack provisions and previous technology sponsorships, underscores our commitment to improving educational outcomes in the community.
