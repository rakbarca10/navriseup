Grateful Hearts, Healthier Communities: Free Medical Camps in Kandivali & Thane

Event Details
  • Date: October 28, 2026
  • Location: Kandivali & Thane, Maharashtra, India
  • Initiative: Free Community Medical & Health Check-up Camps
Bringing Healthcare Closer to Families

Grateful hearts, healthier communities 💛 Navriseup India Care Foundation recently organised free medical camps in Kandivali and Thane, bringing check-ups, consultations, and health guidance closer to families who need them most.

Accessible Check-ups & Consultations

For many underserved families, routine medical check-ups and timely doctor consultations are often delayed due to financial constraints or lack of access. Through these free medical camps in Kandivali and Thane, residents received direct medical attention, preventive screenings, and practical health guidance right in their neighborhood.

Powered by Dedicated Doctors & Volunteers

None of this would have been possible without our incredible doctors, medical staff, volunteers, partners, and every community member who showed up. Thank you for your time, care, and trust. 🙏

  • Preventive Screening: Early identification of common health concerns through on-ground medical check-ups.
  • Expert Guidance: Personalized medical consultations and wellness advice from qualified doctors and healthcare staff.
  • Community Trust: Together, we’re building a healthier tomorrow, one camp at a time.
Support Our Community Healthcare Drives

Help us bring essential medical camps and health awareness to more neighborhoods across Maharashtra:

  • Donate: Sponsor medical supplies, diagnostic kits, and essential medicines for upcoming health camps.
  • Volunteer: Join us as a healthcare professional or ground volunteer during our community drives.
  • Collaborate: Partner with us to organize free health check-up camps in underserved areas.
