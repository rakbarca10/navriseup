Brighter Classrooms, Better Futures: Whiteboard Distribution at ZP School

Event Details
  • Date: January 22, 2026
  • Location: Zilla Parishad School, Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar)
  • Impact: Interactive Classrooms for Students & Educators
Modernizing Rural Classrooms

NavRiseUp India Care Foundation distributed modern whiteboards and classroom teaching supplies to Zilla Parishad school students and teachers in Aurangabad. This drive helps educators transition to clear, dust-free, and interactive teaching methods, making daily lessons significantly more visual and engaging for every child.

Turning Classrooms into Spaces of Curiosity

Traditional chalkboards often produce dust and become uneven over time, affecting children with dust sensitivities and limiting visual presentations. High-quality whiteboards and markers transform ordinary classrooms into dynamic environments where students eagerly come up to solve problems and express their curiosity.

Upgrading Rural Educational Infrastructure

Effective infrastructure is key to bridging the rural-urban schooling divide:

  • Healthier Classrooms: Dust-free teaching surfaces create a cleaner respiratory atmosphere for young children and teachers.
  • Enhanced Pedagogical Visuals: Clear whiteboards allow teachers to draw diagrams, write legible notes, and teach interactively.
  • Encouraging Student Participation: Children take pride in demonstrating their answers on the new boards.
Help Transform More Classrooms
