Senior Citizen Care & Elder Dignity: Supporting Abandoned Seniors in India

Published by: Navriseup Elder Care Desk

In traditional Indian society, elders have long held the honored status of family anchors, mentors, and storytellers. However, rapid urbanization, socio-economic transitions, and nuclear family structures have created a silent crisis: the neglect and abandonment of senior citizens. Stripped of financial savings, battling chronic illnesses, and enduring severe loneliness, hundreds of thousands of elderly individuals live in acute vulnerability.

The Growing Demographic Crisis of India's Elderly

India is home to over 140 million senior citizens (aged 60 and above), a demographic expected to surpass 300 million by 2050. Despite this rapid demographic expansion, social security safety nets remain minimal:

  • Lack of Pension Security: Over 70% of senior citizens worked in the informal sector (agriculture, construction, street vending) and have zero pension or health insurance coverage.
  • Chronic Multimorbidity: Common ailments such as osteoarthritis, hypertension, diabetes, cataract blindness, dementia, and cardiovascular diseases require lifelong medication that destitute seniors cannot afford.
  • Social Isolation & Emotional Neglect: The psychological toll of being isolated, feeling like a burden, or being cast out of family homes causes high rates of depression and cognitive decline among aging citizens.

Key Interventions of Navriseup's Elder Care Drive

At Navriseup India Care Foundation, our Extend a Caring Hand initiative is committed to ensuring that old age is met with dignity, comfort, and unconditional respect:

1. Monthly Geriatric Medicine Sponsorship

For an elderly widow or widower living below the poverty line, choosing between basic groceries and hypertension medication is a heartbreaking monthly reality. Navriseup's medical volunteers review prescriptions and deliver free monthly medicine packages containing essential cardiac, diabetic, arthritis, and ophthalmic medicines directly to senior citizens' homes.

2. Support for Old Age Homes (Vridh Ashram Aid)

Many charitable old-age homes run on shoestring budgets, struggling to purchase clean bed linens, adult diapers, mobility walkers, and hygiene cleansers. We supply these non-profit homes with bulk nutritional groceries (flour, lentils, cooking oil, milk powder, fruits), specialized orthopedic mattresses, and wheelchair equipment.

3. Regular Doctor Visits & Physiotherapy Camps

Because restricted mobility makes traveling to public hospitals painful and expensive, our mobile healthcare team organizes on-site diagnostic camps at shelters and slum clusters. Volunteer physicians evaluate joint health, check vitals, administer pain-management physiotherapy, and arrange cataract surgeries through partner charitable eye hospitals.

4. The Gift of Time: Youth Companionship and Listening Sessions

Medicine heals the body, but compassion heals the heart. Our youth volunteers spend regular weekends with residents of senior homes—reading newspapers together, playing carrom, sharing festive meals on Diwali and New Year, and listening to their cherished memories. Reconnecting elders with eager young listeners revitalizes their sense of self-worth.

Protecting Elder Rights: Legal and Social Safeguards

Under the Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act (2007), parents and grandparents in India have the statutory right to claim maintenance from adult children. Non-governmental organizations assist illiterate and distressed elders in understanding their legal rights, accessing government old-age welfare schemes, and securing safe shelter when living conditions at home become abusive.

How You Can Brighten an Elder's Golden Years
