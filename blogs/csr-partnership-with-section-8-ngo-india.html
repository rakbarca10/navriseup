Corporate CSR Partnerships with Section 8 NGOs in India: Compliance & Impact Guide

Published by: Navriseup Corporate Partnerships Desk

Under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, India became the first country to mandate corporate social responsibility (CSR) spending for qualifying enterprises. Qualifying corporations (having net worth ≥ ₹500 crore, turnover ≥ ₹1,000 crore, or net profit ≥ ₹5 crore) must allocate at least 2% of their average net profits to eligible Schedule VII initiatives.

Why Partner with a Registered Section 8 NGO for CSR Implementation?

While companies can establish captive foundations, partnering with established grassroots implementing agencies offers substantial advantages in compliance, agility, and measurable social returns:

  • Statutory MCA CSR-1 Registration: Implementing agencies must be registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) by filing Form CSR-1. Navriseup India Care Foundation holds full CSR-1, 12A, 80G, and MCA Section 8 credentials (CIN: U88900MH2023NPL407325).
  • Established Field Presence: Direct ground access to verified beneficiaries across urban slums and peri-urban clusters in Maharashtra.
  • Rigorous Financial Transparency: Timely utilization certificates, audited financials, and milestone-linked expenditure tracking.

Key Areas for CSR Project Collaboration Under Schedule VII

1. Eradicating Hunger, Poverty & Malnutrition (Schedule VII item i)

Funding sustainable food grain distribution, community soup kitchens, and emergency disaster relief rations for displaced and distressed families.

2. Promoting Education & Digital Literacy (Schedule VII item ii)

Supporting slum child learning hubs (Chhoti Si Asha), providing smart classroom equipment, school kits, and teacher stipends to boost retention.

3. Gender Equality & Women Empowerment (Schedule VII item iii)

Establishing community vocational tailoring and handicraft centers that train women from low-income households in income-generating skills.

4. Healthcare & Sanitation (Schedule VII item i)

Sponsoring free preventive medical screening camps, pediatric health checkups, and menstrual hygiene awareness drives.

5. Environmental Sustainability & Ecological Balance (Schedule VII item iv)

Corporate employee tree plantation drives, urban greening, and waste cleanup campaigns.

CSR Project Lifecycle with Navriseup

  1. Needs Assessment & Concept Note: Jointly identifying the target demographic, geographic focus, and expected outputs.
  2. MOU & Governance Framework: Formulating clear deliverables, fund disbursement schedules, and key performance indicators (KPIs).
  3. Execution & Monitoring: Real-time field execution with structured photographic and quantitative progress reports.
  4. Impact Assessment & Utilization Certification: Issuing formal Fund Utilization Certificates (UCs) and audited statements for corporate statutory annual filings.
